  • Name ideas↗Renaming Fader: names from Jujutsu Kaisen, Steins;Gate, Re:Zero, Monogatari and Japanese sound words. Otto won.

What it does

  • Mixer flyout

    Click the Windows speaker icon, the tray icon or Ctrl+Alt+V: every app with audio, icon (click = mute), slider, live meter, master on top.

  • Scroll on the taskbar

    Wheel over an app's taskbar button changes its volume, no key held. Over the clock or empty taskbar: master.

  • Alt + scroll anywhere

    Changes the volume of the app under the mouse, borderless games included. Modifier is configurable.

  • Start level

    When an app opens its audio, Otto sets it to your start level (50 % by default). Per-app rules give some apps their own level or leave them alone.

  • Level pill

    A small pill shows the level at a fixed spot: top, center or bottom of the screen the mouse is on.

  • Louder than 100 %

    Sliders go to 150, 200 or 300 %. FxSound lifts the sound and Otto turns the other apps down to match, so only the boosted app is louder. Master above 100 % boosts every app.

  • FxSound card

    In settings: get FxSound, turn it on or off, pick its preset, set its five effects and its speakers.

  • Portable

    Drop an empty portable file next to the exe and settings stay in that folder, handy on a USB stick.

Good to know

  • Admin apps

    Apps running as administrator don't get the scroll gestures unless Otto runs elevated. The mixer still controls them.

  • Exclusive full-screen games

    Old DirectX full-screen hides every overlay, so no pill. The volume still changes.

  • Volume is not loudness

    A per-app leveler was tried and removed: Windows' per-app meter can't tell speech from a loud video. Use Windows' Loudness Equalization if you want everything flattened.

    Newv0.6.0: Otto × FxSound

    0.5.0's boost gave static through VoiceMeeter, so boost now goes through FxSound: app at 200 % = FxSound +6 dB and every other app halved. No rerouting, no delay. Settings get an FxSound card (install button, on/off, presets, effects, speakers). Silent test 17/17. The spare-output boost is now opt-in.

    Per-app and master sliders can go past 100 % (up to 150, 200 or 300 %, in Settings → Boost). Uses a spare output (VoiceMeeter Input, VB-CABLE…) and puts every app back when you go under 100 % or quit, even after a crash. Apps that ignore the move stay capped at 100 %. Tested silently 19/19. Released and installed on your PC.
